Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. PlateLog has no accounts and no sign-up — just open the app and start logging.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. PlateLog is fully offline and has no servers, so your profile, goals, and food logs never leave your phone.

How do I reset or delete my data?

Open the Profile tab and tap “Reset all data” to erase everything. Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

The search can't find a food I want.

PlateLog searches a built-in food list and your own custom foods offline, and pulls in thousands more from OpenFoodFacts when you're online. You can always add your own item with “Add my own food” in the Search tab.

How are my calorie and macro goals set?

You can enter them yourself in the Profile tab, or use the built-in TDEE calculator to estimate a daily target from your age, weight, height, and activity level.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline. Only the online food search needs a connection; the rest of the app runs entirely on your device.
